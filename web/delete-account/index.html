Delete your Lock In account

This page is for Lock In: Finance Tracker by Andrija Dmitrović. You only have an account if you signed in (for shared goals with friends or your subscription).

In the app

More > Settings > Danger zone > Delete account, then tap it again to confirm. It happens at once.

Without the app

Write to support@lockintracker.app from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What gets deleted

Your account and email address, your display name, friend requests and friendships, nudges, push tokens, your trial and subscription record, your membership in every shared goal and your payments into them. Goals you were alone in are deleted; goals with other people stay theirs, without you.

What is not deleted

  • The spending on your phone: it was never on our server. Delete it with More > Settings > Reset everything, or by deleting the app.
  • Your App Store or Google Play subscription: deleting the account does not cancel it. Cancel it in your phone’s subscription settings (see Support).
  • Technical logs at our hosting provider (for example IP address and time of a request) are deleted on its own schedule; purchase records at Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept as the law requires for payments.
