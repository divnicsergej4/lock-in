1. What Lock In is

Lock In helps you track your spending, keep a streak, plan your month and save for goals, alone or with friends. It is a tool for your own notes and habits. It is not financial, legal or tax advice, and it does not connect to your bank. The numbers are only what you enter.

2. The messages

Lock In comments on your spending, sometimes harshly (you choose how harshly in Settings: Mild, Normal or No mercy). The messages are meant as humour and motivation, not as a judgement of you.

An account is only needed for shared goals and your subscription. You sign in with a code sent to your email, with Apple or with Google. Keep access to that email or account to yourself. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Danger zone > Delete account.

5. Friends and shared goals

Lock In limits how many invites, goals and payments can be made in a time, to stop abuse. We may suspend or delete accounts that break these rules.
