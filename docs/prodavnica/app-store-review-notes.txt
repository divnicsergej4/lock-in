2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Lock In is a personal expense tracker for young adults (students and people early in their careers) who overspend and find budgeting apps boring. You log a purchase in a few taps and the app replies with a short, honest and sometimes harsh message; you choose how harsh: Gentle, Normal or No mercy. A daily streak, weekly challenges, envelopes, savings goals, charts and a monthly report help build the habit of tracking money. Shared goals let friends save together for one thing, for example a trip. There is no bank connection: amounts, notes and categories stay on the device.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
- Without an account: on the plans screen tap "Start my free week" (7 days free in sandbox, then an auto-renewable monthly or yearly subscription). Then every feature is available: Home (log an expense or income), Spending (charts, all transactions, limits), Savings (envelopes, monthly plan), More (monthly report, settings).
- Demo account (Sign-In Information above): on the plans screen tap "Already have an account? Sign in", then "Continue with Google". This account already has full access, no purchase needed.
- Shared goals (Savings tab) need an account: sign in with Apple, Google or a 6-digit code sent by email. A new account is created at the first sign-in. Friends are added by email invite and must accept it.
- "Look around the demo first" on the plans screen fills every screen with sample data (read only).
- Delete account: More > Settings > Danger zone > Delete account (tap twice).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (EU): accounts, sign-in and the data of shared goals (friends, goals, contributions)
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: sign-in
- Resend: emails with the sign-in code and friend invites
- Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection on the sign-in screen
- Cloudflare Pages: our website (privacy policy, terms, support)
- App Store In-App Purchase and RevenueCat: subscriptions and the free trial
- Expo (EAS): push notifications for shared goals and app updates
No AI services, ads, analytics, or bank and financial data providers.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in all regions. It is in English only. Prices follow the App Store's local pricing.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY
Not applicable. Lock In is a personal budgeting tool: no bank connection, no payments or money transfers between users, no lending, investing or financial advice, and no protected third-party content.

USER CONTENT
Nothing is public. A shared goal (its name, contributions and their short notes) is visible only to friends the user invited by email and who accepted the invite. A user can leave any goal at any time, which removes their contributions, and can delete their account.
