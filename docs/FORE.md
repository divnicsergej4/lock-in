## 2. Special situations (these win over the category line)
- **Balance goes below zero:** bro, you're in the red. stop. 🥀🥀🥀
- **One expense is over 30% of everything the user has:** that's over 30% of everything you have, are you really sure????
- **Third expense of the day (once a day):** third expense today bro...lock your phone 🥀
- **Whole monthly income spent:** you've spent your whole monthly income bro. stop it. now.
- **Over 80% of monthly income spent:** over 80% of your monthly income is already gone 😭😭😭✌️
- **Over a category limit:** you went over your monthly limit for {category} ({spent} / {limit}) 🥀🥀
- **Close to a limit (80%+):** you're close to your limit for {category}: {spent} / {limit} 🥀

## 3. Roast level (Settings)

- **Normal:** the lines above, without the [No mercy] ones.
- **No mercy:** normal lines + [No mercy] lines (~70%), and one of these is added at the end:
  - Not even your mom would approve.
